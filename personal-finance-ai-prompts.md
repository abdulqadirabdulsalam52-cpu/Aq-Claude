## 1. Income Stream Blueprint

```
Act as a personal finance strategist. I want to create multiple sources of income and become less dependent on a single income stream.

Analyze my current income sources: [list]
Available capital: [amount]
Available time per week: [hours]

Develop 7 potential income streams that match my situation, such as salary, investments, side businesses, dividends, rental income, digital products, affiliate marketing, or freelance services.

For each income stream, provide:

- Startup difficulty
- Initial investment required
- Estimated time to generate income
- Risk level
- Growth potential
- Suggested implementation order

Finally, create a 12-month action plan and a practical checklist to help make my finances more stable and resilient.
```

## 2. Debt Elimination Engine

```
Act as a debt repayment specialist.

Here are my current debts: [list with balance, interest rate, and minimum payment]
Extra amount available for monthly debt payments: [amount]

Compare these three repayment approaches:

Snowball: Focus on paying off the smallest balance first.

Avalanche: Prioritize the debt with the highest interest rate first.

Hybrid: Combine the benefits of motivation from quick wins with potential interest savings.

For each strategy, calculate:

- Repayment order
- Suggested monthly payment
- Estimated total interest
- Projected debt-free date
- Key advantages
- Potential disadvantages

Then explain which approach best fits my specific financial situation and why.

Finish with a clear month-by-month repayment plan and an easy-to-use debt tracking table.
```

## 3. Expense Leak Detector

```
Act as a personal expense auditor.

I want to uncover hidden spending leaks in my monthly budget.

Analyze these expenses: [paste expenses or categories]
Household size: [number of people]
Monthly income: [amount]

Identify unnecessary or avoidable spending across:

- Forgotten subscriptions
- Food delivery
- Impulse purchases
- Overpriced bills
- Bank fees
- Insurance costs
- Energy expenses
- Transportation
- Entertainment
- Small recurring charges

For each spending leak, evaluate it based on:

- Potential monthly savings
- Difficulty of eliminating it
- Impact on my quality of life
- Priority level

Finally, create a practical 30-day cost-cutting plan that helps reduce my expenses while keeping my lifestyle comfortable and sustainable.
```

## 4. Shame-Free Budget

```
Act as a practical personal finance coach.

I want to build a budgeting system that I can realistically follow—without shame, guilt, or pressure from others.

My monthly income: [amount]
My fixed monthly expenses: [amount]
My biggest spending trigger: [description]

Create a personalized budget using the 50/30/20 rule, while adapting it to my actual financial situation.

Include:

- Recommended budget allocation
- Spending limits for each category
- A simple weekly budget review routine
- Guidelines for enjoying leisure spending without guilt
- Polite ways to turn down expensive plans without feeling uncomfortable

Finally, provide 5 realistic scripts I can use in social situations, including dinners, trips, birthdays, group purchases, and last-minute plans.
```

## 5. Salary Negotiation Script Generator

```
Act as a salary negotiation specialist.

My role: [job title]
Company or industry: [company/industry]
Years of experience: [number]
Current salary or job offer: [amount]
Target salary: [amount]

Create 3 practical negotiation scripts for:

1. Asking for a salary increase
2. Making a counteroffer after receiving a job offer
3. Responding when the employer says, "There's no budget."

For each situation, include:

- The strongest negotiation points to use
- Market data and salary information I should research beforehand
- Exact phrases I can say
- Possible objections from the employer
- Effective responses to each objection
- Alternative benefits if the salary cannot be increased, such as a bonus, remote work, training budget, additional vacation, equity, or a salary review within 90 days

Finally, create a 1-hour practice routine to help me prepare confidently before the negotiation.
```

## 6. Impulse Spending Blocker

```
Act as a financial behavior systems designer.

I want to reduce impulse purchases and become more intentional with my spending.

My biggest spending triggers are: [description]
I use my credit card this often: [frequency]
The categories where I tend to overspend are: [list]

Create a practical 30-day system that adds friction and encourages me to pause before making a purchase.

Include:

- A waiting-period rule for non-essential purchases
- A flexible weekly spending limit
- A simple wishlist system
- A process for reviewing and canceling unnecessary subscriptions
- A clear rule for online shopping
- A strategy for handling emotional spending
- A decision-making checklist to help determine whether I should buy something

The goal isn't to completely stop spending—it's to help me spend intentionally and make better purchasing decisions.
```

## 7. Automatic Savings System

```
Act as a personal financial planner.

I want to create an automated savings system that works consistently without depending on willpower.

My monthly net income: [amount]
My fixed monthly expenses: [amount]
Current savings: [amount]
Primary financial goal: [emergency fund / travel / investing / home purchase / debt repayment]

Design a personalized savings hierarchy that includes:

- My initial emergency savings target
- A long-term emergency fund covering 3–6 months of expenses
- A recommended percentage of income to save automatically
- The ideal transfer schedule
- Suitable account types for each savings goal
- Rules to prevent unnecessary withdrawals
- A strategy to gradually increase my savings rate over time

Also include projections for small, moderate, and aggressive monthly contributions so I can see how each approach could affect my progress.
```

## 8. Side Hustle Validator

```
Act as an expert in building and evaluating additional income streams.

I want to test a side hustle idea before investing too much time, effort, or money into it.

My skills: [list]
Hours available per week: [number]
Starting budget: [amount]
Side hustle ideas I'm considering: [list]

Evaluate 5 potential ideas using a decision matrix that considers:

- Ease of getting started
- Time to first income
- Market demand
- Level of competition
- Potential profit margins
- Scalability
- Skills required
- Risk level

After evaluating them, identify the most suitable option for my situation and create a practical 30-day launch plan designed to help me get my first client or make my first sale.
```

```
My financial goals: [goals]

Design a dashboard that tracks:

- Income
- Expenses
- Savings
- Debt
- Net worth
- Investments
- Subscriptions
- Fees and charges
- Progress toward my financial goals

Specify which metrics I should review:

Weekly: [metrics]
Monthly: [metrics]
Quarterly: [metrics]

Also create a simple 15-minute weekly financial check-in that helps me stay organized and in control of my money without becoming overly focused on it.
```

## 10. Financial Mindset Rewriter

```
Act as a financial habits coach.

I want to improve my relationship with money and stop making financial decisions from a place of scarcity, guilt, or chaos.

My current income: [amount]
My biggest limiting belief about money: [description]
My most harmful financial habit: [description]

Create a practical 21-day financial reset plan that includes:

- A daily reflection
- One small action to complete each day
- A positive financial habit to reinforce
- One intentional spending decision
- A gratitude or financial clarity exercise
- One simple metric to track my progress

Avoid generic motivational quotes.

I want a practical, psychology-informed system that helps me develop healthier money habits and make better financial decisions every day.
```
